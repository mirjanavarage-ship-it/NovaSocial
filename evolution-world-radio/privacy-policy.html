Privacy Policy for EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO

Last updated: August 12, 2026

EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO is an internet radio application that lets users discover and listen to online radio stations from around the world. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

1. Information processed by the app

The app may store settings and usage preferences locally on your device, such as your selected country, favorite stations, listening history, theme, volume level, and other app preferences. This local data is used to provide app functionality and restore your settings.

2. Internet radio services

When you search for or play radio stations, the app connects to third-party radio directories, station servers, and streaming providers. Those third parties may receive technical information normally transmitted when making an internet request, such as your IP address, device/network information, and request details. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interactions, diagnostics, and other data needed to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising, depending on your region and consent choices.

Where required by law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices before certain advertising data is processed. You can change available privacy choices later from the app when that option is provided.

4. Google services

Google's handling of information for advertising is described in Google's privacy and advertising documentation. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by third-party service providers that are necessary to operate features of the app, including radio directory/streaming providers and advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

6. Data retention

App preferences stored locally remain on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or the app removes them as part of normal operation. Data processed by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Children

EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO is not specifically directed to children under 13. If the app is distributed to audiences that include children, additional Google Play and advertising requirements may apply.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures within the app, but no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure. Third-party streams and services are outside our direct control.

9. Your choices

You can stop use of the app at any time, clear app data through Android settings, remove favorites/history within the app where available, and use privacy choices presented by Google UMP when required.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, advertising technology, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at this URL with an updated revision date.
