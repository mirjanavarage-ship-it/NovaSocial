# Privacy Policy for EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

**Developer:** EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO  
**Privacy contact:** mirjanavarage@gmail.com

EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO is an internet radio application that lets users discover and listen to online radio stations from around the world. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information processed by the app

The app stores settings and usage preferences locally on your device, such as your selected country, favorite stations, listening history, theme, volume level, cached station lists, and other app preferences. This local data is used to provide app functionality and restore your settings. Android application backup is disabled for this release.

## 2. Internet radio services

When you search for or play radio stations, the app connects to third-party radio directories, station servers, and streaming providers. Those third parties may receive technical information normally transmitted when making an internet request, such as your IP address, device/network information, and request details. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies. Some independent radio streams may use HTTP rather than HTTPS.

## 3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display a banner advertisement. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interactions, diagnostics, and other data needed to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising, depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's advertising controls.

Where required by law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present privacy choices before certain advertising data is processed. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, it is available from the app's **About & Privacy** screen.

## 4. Google services

Google's handling of information for advertising is described in Google's privacy and advertising documentation:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information for advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by third-party service providers that are necessary to operate features of the app, including radio directory/streaming providers and advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

## 6. Permissions and background playback

The app uses internet/network access, wake lock, and a media-playback foreground service so user-initiated radio can continue playing in the background with Android media controls. The app does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, call-log, or storage/media-library permissions.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

App preferences stored locally remain on your device until you clear the app's storage/data, remove them through available app controls, or uninstall the app. The app does not maintain a developer-operated user account database. Data processed by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies and legal obligations.

You can delete locally stored app data at any time through Android settings by clearing the app's storage/data or by uninstalling the app.

## 8. Children

EVOLUTION WORLD RADIO is not specifically directed to children under 13. The Google Play target-audience declaration must match the audiences actually selected for distribution. If a future release is intentionally directed to children, the app, advertising configuration, and this policy must be reviewed for applicable Families requirements before that release.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures within the app, but no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure. Google Mobile Ads transmits its SDK data using TLS. Independent third-party radio streams and services are outside our direct control.

## 10. Your privacy choices

You can stop using the app at any time, clear app data through Android settings, remove favorites/history within the app where available, and use privacy choices presented by Google UMP when required. The app does not require an account.

## 11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app functionality, advertising technology, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at this URL with an updated revision date.
